About Samyak

Centre for Sanskrit Heritage & Knowledge

Dedicated to preserving, studying, and celebrating the timeless wisdom of Sanskrit and Indian heritage.

Our Vision

To create a world where Sanskrit language and heritage are recognized, respected, and actively engaged with by people of all backgrounds. We envision a thriving global community dedicated to understanding and preserving the intellectual and spiritual treasures of Sanskrit civilization.

Our Mission

To make Sanskrit language, philosophy, and cultural practices accessible through modern educational methods. We are committed to bridging ancient wisdom with contemporary knowledge systems and fostering appreciation for India's intellectual heritage.

Our History & Journey

2015

Samyak was founded with the vision of creating a modern center for Sanskrit heritage preservation and education. We started with a small team of passionate Sanskrit scholars and educators.

2017

Launched our first comprehensive Sanskrit curriculum and established partnerships with educational institutions. Our programs began attracting students from across the country.

2019

Expanded to international students with online programs. We began digitizing ancient manuscripts and making them available for research and study.

2023 - Present

Operating as a leading center with multiple programs, publications, and a growing global community. We continue to innovate in Sanskrit education while maintaining deep respect for traditional methods.
