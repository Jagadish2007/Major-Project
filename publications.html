Research & Articles

Access our research papers, scholarly articles, and publications on Sanskrit and heritage studies

  • The Evolution of Sanskrit Grammar Systems: From Vedic to Classical

    Published on November 15, 2024 | Research Article

    A comprehensive study examining the development of Sanskrit grammar from Vedic period through classical texts. This research explores Panini's revolutionary grammar system, comparative analysis with other linguistic traditions, and the influence on modern linguistic theory. The paper includes detailed analysis of grammatical evolution patterns.

    Authors: Dr. Rajesh Kumar, Prof. Priya Sharma
    Pages: 45 | Language: English

    Download PDF

  • Philosophical Insights from the Upanishads: Brahman, Atman, and Reality

    Published on October 28, 2024 | Scholarly Essay

    An in-depth exploration of fundamental philosophical concepts presented in the Upanishads, particularly focusing on the concept of Brahman (ultimate reality), Atman (individual self), and their relationship. This essay bridges ancient Sanskrit philosophy with contemporary metaphysical discussions, making it relevant to both scholars and practitioners.

    Author: Dr. Vedant Tripathi
    Pages: 28 | Language: English

    Download PDF

  • Vedic Mathematics: Ancient Computational Methods and Modern Applications

    Published on September 12, 2024 | Technical Paper

    Detailed analysis of mathematical techniques preserved in Vedic texts and their practical applications in contemporary mathematics. Discover how ancient Sanskrit scholars developed sophisticated computational systems including algebraic techniques, geometry, and astronomical calculations. Includes demonstrations and proofs of various Vedic mathematical sutras.

    Authors: Prof. Amit Patel, Dr. Kavya Sharma
    Pages: 52 | Language: English, Sanskrit

    Download PDF

  • Manuscript Preservation and Digitization: Best Practices in Heritage Conservation

    Published on August 5, 2024 | Technical Guide

    Comprehensive guide on modern techniques for preserving and digitizing ancient manuscripts. This publication covers assessment methodologies, digital imaging standards, metadata requirements, and long-term preservation strategies. Includes case studies from Samyak's manuscript digitization project and recommendations for institutions worldwide.

    Authors: Amit Patel, Dr. Anita Desai, UNESCO Heritage Team
    Pages: 78 | Language: English

    Download PDF

  • The Role of Sanskrit in Modern Education: Cognitive and Cultural Benefits

    Published on July 20, 2024 | Educational Research

    Empirical study examining the cognitive and cultural benefits of Sanskrit education for modern learners. Based on survey data from over 2,000 Samyak students, this research demonstrates improvements in linguistic ability, cognitive development, and cultural awareness. The paper includes recommendations for integrating Sanskrit into educational curricula.

    Authors: Prof. Priya Sharma, Dr. Rajesh Kumar, Dr. Elena Rodriguez
    Pages: 35 | Language: English

    Download PDF

  • Comparative Analysis of Sanskrit and Indo-European Language Families

    Published on June 15, 2024 | Linguistic Research

    Detailed comparative study of Sanskrit and other Indo-European languages, examining linguistic similarities, divergences, and evolutionary pathways. This research contributes to understanding of language families and proto-languages, with implications for historical linguistics and anthropological studies.

    Author: Dr. Marcus Friedrich
    Pages: 64 | Language: English, Sanskrit

    Download PDF

  • Ayurveda and Sanskrit: Traditional Knowledge Systems in Practice

    Published on May 28, 2024 | Interdisciplinary Study

    Exploration of the deep connections between Sanskrit language and Ayurvedic medicine, examining how the structure and vocabulary of Sanskrit reflects and supports Ayurvedic principles. Includes practical applications and modern interpretations of classical Ayurvedic texts written in Sanskrit.

    Authors: Dr. Vaidya Ashok, Prof. Priya Sharma
    Pages: 41 | Language: English, Sanskrit

    Download PDF

  • Digital Humanities and Sanskrit Studies: Technology in Service of Ancient Texts

    Published on April 10, 2024 | Technology Review

    Overview of cutting-edge technologies being applied to Sanskrit studies including OCR for Devanagari script, natural language processing, digital textual analysis tools, and virtual platforms for collaborative research. Features interviews with scholars using these technologies and recommendations for future development.

    Author: Amit Patel
    Pages: 38 | Language: English

    Download PDF

  • Classical Sanskrit Poetry: Analysis of Kavya Literature and Literary Devices

    Published on March 22, 2024 | Literary Analysis

    Comprehensive analysis of classical Sanskrit poetry (Kavya literature), examining masterworks like Kalidasa's Shakuntala and Maghanataka. The paper details literary devices, aesthetic principles (Rasa theory), and the evolution of poetic styles across different periods of Sanskrit literature.

    Authors: Prof. Anjali Singh, Dr. Vedant Tripathi
    Pages: 56 | Language: English, Sanskrit

    Download PDF
